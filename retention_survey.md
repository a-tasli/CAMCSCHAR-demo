### Data Quality & Participant Attention Verification Protocol

* **Purpose:** To detect and screen out participants who engaged in "survey satisficing" (skimming questions, straightlining, rapid clicking without reading, or selecting default options).
* **Attention Check Scoring (Separate from Knowledge Score):**
  * **Attention Score:** Scored out of 2 points (1 point for Q11 [Option C], 1 point for Q18 [Option B]).
* **Participant Retention Criteria:**
  * **Pass (2/2):** High attention and data fidelity. Included in primary sample ($N=30$).
  * **Borderline (1/2):** Retained in main analysis but flagged for sensitivity analysis to verify whether results hold with or without them.
  * **Fail (0/2):** Systematic inattention / prompt neglect. Data must be excluded from final statistical comparisons and replaced with an attentive participant.

---

### Scoring Rubric & Sub-Metrics

1. **Retention Item Scoring (Excludes Q11 and Q18):**
   * Correct Answer: **+1 point**
   * Incorrect Distractor: **0 points** (logged as `Incorrect Guess`)
   * "I don't remember / Not sure" (Option E): **0 points** (logged as `Omission / Uncertainty`)

2. **Sub-Scores to Compute per Participant:**
   * **Core Retention Score (Total: /12):**
     * Amphitheatre Core Score (Q1–Q4) (/4) — *Baseline (Low CL)*
     * Lake Memorial Core Score (Q7–Q10) (/4) — *Medium CL*
     * Tosun Terzioğlu Garden Core Score (Q14–Q17) (/4) — *High CL / Overload*
   * **Detailed Retention Score (Total: /6):**
     * Amphitheatre Detailed (Q5, Q6) (/2)
     * Lake Memorial Detailed (Q12, Q13) (/2)
     * Tosun Terzioğlu Garden Detailed (Q19, Q20) (/2)
   * **Overall Retention Score (Total: /18)**
   * **Omission Rate:** $\frac{\text{Count of Option E selections across 18 retention items}}{18} \times 100\%$
   * **Error Rate:** $\frac{\text{Count of incorrect distractor selections across 18 retention items}}{18} \times 100\%$

### Statistical Hypothesis Testing Plan

1. **Primary Hypothesis (Retention under Cognitive Load):**
   * **Prediction:** In the High Cognitive Load phase (Tosun Terzioğlu Garden: Q14–Q17), participants in the **Auto Mode** will achieve significantly higher Core Retention scores than participants in the **Static Mode** ($p < 0.05$, independent samples t-test or Mann-Whitney U test), because the automated system reduced visual clutter and simplified auditory information density.
   * **Equivalence Check:** In the Low Cognitive Load baseline phase (Amphitheatre: Q1–Q4), both groups are predicted to exhibit similar retention scores ($p > 0.05$).

2. **Secondary Hypothesis (Detailed script capture vs. overload):**
   * Evaluates whether Static participants in high-load segments successfully absorbed detailed facts (Q19, Q20) or suffered general degradation of both core and detailed knowledge due to auditory/visual saturation.

3. **Subjective Workload (NASA-TLX):**
   * **Prediction:** Overall NASA-TLX workload, especially *Mental Demand*, *Temporal Demand*, and *Frustration*, will be significantly lower in the **Auto Mode** group.
