### Scoring Rubric & Sub-Metrics

1. **Item Scoring:**
   * Correct Answer: **+1 point**
   * Incorrect Distractor: **0 points** (logged as `Incorrect Guess`)
   * "I don't remember / Not sure" (Option E): **0 points** (logged as `Omission / Uncertainty`)

2. **Sub-Scores to Compute per Participant:**
   * **Core Retention Score (Total: /12):**
     * Amphitheatre Core Score (/4) — *Baseline (Low CL)*
     * Lake Memorial Core Score (/4) — *Medium CL*
     * Tosun Terzioğlu Garden Core Score (/4) — *High CL / Overload*
   * **Detailed Retention Score (Total: /6):**
     * Amphitheatre Detailed (/2)
     * Lake Memorial Detailed (/2)
     * Tosun Terzioğlu Garden Detailed (/2)
   * **Overall Retention Score (Total: /18)**
   * **Omission Rate:** $\frac{\text{Count of Option E selections}}{18} \times 100\%$
   * **Error Rate:** $\frac{\text{Count of incorrect distractor selections}}{18} \times 100\%$

### Statistical Hypothesis Testing Plan

1. **Primary Hypothesis (Retention under Cognitive Load):**
   * **Prediction:** In the High Cognitive Load phase (Tosun Terzioğlu Garden), participants in the **Auto Mode** will achieve significantly higher Core Retention scores than participants in the **Static Mode** ($p < 0.05$, independent samples t-test or Mann-Whitney U test), because the automated system reduced visual clutter and simplified auditory information density.
   * **Equivalence Check:** In the Low Cognitive Load baseline phase (Amphitheatre), both groups are predicted to exhibit similar retention scores ($p > 0.05$).

2. **Secondary Hypothesis (Detailed script capture vs. overload):**
   * Evaluates whether Static participants in high-load segments successfully absorbed detailed facts (Q17, Q18) or suffered general degradation of both core and detailed knowledge due to auditory/visual saturation.

3. **Subjective Workload (NASA-TLX):**
   * **Prediction:** Overall NASA-TLX workload, especially *Mental Demand*, *Temporal Demand*, and *Frustration*, will be significantly lower in the **Auto Mode** group.
