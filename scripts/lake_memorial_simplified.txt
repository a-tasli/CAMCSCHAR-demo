Now we arrive at the beautiful campus lake in the middle of the university.
Before 1997, this area was just an empty, dusty gravel pit.  
Builders smoothed the land, added clay, and covered it with gravel to make this artificial lake.
The lake is filled using a smart system that collects rainwater from the roofs of campus buildings.
It also uses treated wastewater, which provides enough water to keep the campus green even during dry summers.
The lake is now full of life, including fish that grew from eggs dropped by passing seagulls.
A popular running track wraps all the way around the water.  
Right next to this path, you will find the impressive Alumni Wall.
During graduation, students celebrate by taking part in a special brick-laying event.
Every graduate places a brick in the wall to leave their permanent mark on the campus.
This tradition started with the very first graduating class.
The moving water and the solid bricks create a beautiful visual contrast.
The water shows the passing of time, while the bricks show permanent success.
You can walk along the path and easily read the names of past students.
The lake and the memorial highlight the university's love for nature and its students.
