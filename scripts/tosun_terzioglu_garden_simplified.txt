[1] Welcome to the Tosun Terzioğlu Garden, a large area filled with many different plants, named after the school's first leader.
[2] Tosun Terzioğlu was a great university president who loved nature so much that he memorized the scientific names of the trees on campus.
[3] This beautiful garden is a great example of how a damaged piece of land was turned into a healthy, green environment.
[4] Before the school was built in 1997, this large area in Tuzla was just a dusty, empty gravel pit with almost no plants.
[5] Over many years of careful planting and soil work, this dry wasteland was transformed into a thick, green plant sanctuary.
[6] Today, the campus is home to over 170,000 individual plants, including over 100 local types, 150 new types, and 350 variations.
[7] The layout of the garden was planned very carefully to be a beautiful, water-saving landscape that smells and looks great.
[8] Tall trees that lose their leaves, like the large Oriental Plane and Sycamore Maple, are planted to give lots of shade during the hot summer.
[9] In the sunny, outer areas, you can find tough plants that don't need much water, like the prickly Cactus and the tall Pampas Grass.
[10] Closer to the ground, there are many sweet-smelling bushes, like the Autumn Sage and Lavender, which make the air smell nice all year.
[11] As you walk around, you might see the Pink Carpet Weed, a small plant that covers the ground with pretty pink flowers in the summer.
[12] You will also spot the Japanese Mock Orange bush with its shiny green leaves, and the Winter Jasmine, a vine that survives freezing winters.
[13] The garden is full of bright colors from special plants like the red Crimson Bottlebrush and the Queen Elizabeth Rose, famous for its big pink flowers.
[14] There are also very unique trees, like the fast-growing Empress Tree with giant leaves, and the Goldenrain Tree that has yellow flowers.
[15] Plants that stay green all year, like the creeping Juniper and the Bay Laurel, keep the garden looking alive even in the cold winter.
[16] The huge mix of plants, from the tiny Sea Pink flowers to the giant Cedar trees, creates a healthy and balanced nature system.
[17] The Tosun Terzioğlu Garden is more than just plants; it is a special place that respects the university's history and its care for the earth.
[18] Keeping track of all these plants turns the garden into an outdoor science lab, which is very helpful for the school's plant study classes.
[19] Students and scientists can easily watch how different plants, like the Chinese Photinia and the Cape Marguerite, react to the weather outside.
[20] Bringing in new, foreign plants was done very carefully so they would mix well and not harm the local plants already growing there.
[21] The changing seasons in the garden look amazing, especially when the Guelder Rose leaves turn bright red in the fall.
[22] Even the dirt in the garden was specially mixed with nutrients so that all kinds of plants, like the English Yew, can grow perfectly.
[23] By working so hard to grow many plant types, Sabancı University has built a living book of nature that shows how strong the environment can be.
