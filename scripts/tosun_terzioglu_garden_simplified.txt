Beyond the lake, the campus turns into a massive garden containing over 170,000 different plants.  
The most special area here is the Tosun Terzioğlu Garden, named after the university's first rector.
Tosun Terzioğlu loved nature deeply and knew the Latin names of all the trees on campus.
The campus is most famous for having the largest collection of Japanese Sakura trees in Turkey.  
A Japanese company named Komatsu gifted these beautiful cherry blossom saplings between 2007 and 2009.  
Today, nearly 2,000 Sakura trees of seven different types grow beautifully here.  
Just like in Japan, these trees bloom with bright pink and white flowers every March and April.  
Nearby, you will see the Sakura Stone Monument, a huge 3.3-ton rock brought all the way from Japan.  
This rock is 10,000 years old and was shaped by river water to symbolize strength and survival.  
The stone features the word "Sakura" carved into it by a famous Japanese artist.  
Besides the Sakura, you can spot the Judas Tree, which grows bright pink flowers on bare branches in spring.  
Huge Plane and Maple trees offer plenty of cool shade for walking students during the hot summer.  
In the sunny spots, you can smell the lovely scents of Lavender and Autumn Sage plants.  
You will also see tall Pampas Grass blowing in the wind, staying green almost all year long.  
All these different plants make the campus feel like a beautiful, living nature museum.
