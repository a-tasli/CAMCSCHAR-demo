[1] Welcome to the Sabancı University amphitheater, a large and beautiful outdoor space for art and culture.
[2] It is located right next to the Kamil Koç Bekdemir building and the Arts and Social Sciences workshops.
[3] This space was planned in 2021 to bring more art into the outdoor areas of the campus.
[4] You can see the "living stairs," which are special seats that mix plants and nature with the building structure.
[5] There are also falling water features that look beautiful and sound very relaxing.
[6] The tall columns around the edges are designed to look like a traditional Turkish school courtyard, mixing old and new styles.
[7] This design makes the space feel like a friendly place for students to meet, talk, and learn together.
[8] In the center, there are two raised stages built so that different outdoor shows can happen at the same time.
[9] This setup allows for many exciting performances, like plays and concerts, by students and professional artists.
[10] The amphitheater is a great addition to the Arts and Social Sciences faculty, supporting their focus on art and design.
[11] It works together with indoor art galleries on campus by giving students a large outdoor stage to show their work.
[12] With its mix of nature and history, this theater truly shows the university's goal of creating things together.
[13] Using this space every day helps students learn by experiencing things outside the normal classroom.
[14] It removes the walls between studying and the real world, so students can discuss ideas openly.
[15] Also, the strong build of the theater matches the rest of the campus, which was made to survive big earthquakes.
[16] Filled with student energy, the theater is often used for quick debates, fun festivals, and group study times.
[17] When it gets dark, special lights shine on the water and columns, making the theater look like a movie scene.
[18] This design choice ignores old-fashioned, powerful buildings and instead focuses on giving students a place to speak and share.
