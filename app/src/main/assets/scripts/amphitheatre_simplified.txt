Welcome to the Sabancı University Outdoor Amphitheatre.
Built in 2003, this large venue covers an impressive 5,300 square meters.
The design perfectly follows the natural slope and shape of the land.
It can hold 4,000 seated guests and 2,500 standing people.
The theater hosts many concerts, plays, and spring festivals throughout the year.
Its most important event is the annual graduation ceremony.
All students gather here to receive their diplomas together in one place.
The curved seating provides great sound and clear views for everyone in the audience.
You can find it on the eastern side of the campus near the Performing Arts Center.
The design is heavily inspired by ancient open-air theaters found across Turkey.
Evening lights create a beautiful and dramatic mood during nighttime events.
The theater brings together students and the local people of Istanbul.
It is a place meant for sharing experiences and having fun together.
During the day, it is also a quiet and peaceful spot for studying.
This theater is a wonderful place for celebrating both art and academic success.
